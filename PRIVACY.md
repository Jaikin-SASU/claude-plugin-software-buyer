# Privacy policy — Software Buyer France plugin and `prix-logiciel` MCP server

Last updated: 24 September 2026. Controller: JAIKIN SASU, 24 rue de l'Industrie, 67640 Fegersheim, France — contact: victor@jaikin.eu.

## What the plugin does with your documents
Skills and commands run inside your Claude client. Specifications, quotes and contracts you ask Claude to review are **not sent to the MCP server** and are not collected by JAIKIN.

## What the MCP server receives
Only the arguments of the tool calls Claude makes: a project type, optional keywords, a department code, a year, a nature of contract, or an amount to position. No account, no authentication, no cookies.

## Data collection and storage
- The server does **not log or store** tool arguments or responses.
- The hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes technical request metadata (IP address, timestamps, request size) for security and aggregate analytics, under its own privacy policy. JAIKIN does not use these data to identify users.
- No conversation data, memory, chat history or files are requested or accessed.

## Third-party sharing
None. JAIKIN does not sell or share data.

## Retention
No application-level retention. Provider technical logs follow Cloudflare's retention settings.

## Data served
Public procurement data (DECP, data.economie.gouv.fr, Etalab 2.0 licence) contains company identifiers (SIRET) of public buyers and awardees, not personal data about you. Private-market figures are published prices with their public sources.

## Your rights
For any question or GDPR request: victor@jaikin.eu.
